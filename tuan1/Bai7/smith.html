LSM400 Linear Smith Machine

Overview

Linear Smith Machines are the ultimate in weight machines and the LSM400 leads the field. Designed for both serious fitness users and beginners, the LSM400's vertical design provides all of the safety and control associated with Smith Machines, while still allowing for the effective use of gravity for resistance. You don't even need a workout partner.

Features

  • Smooth Smith Action: Bar rated at 800 lb., slides on Teflon bushings.
  • Proper lifting angle: 7 degrees from vertical to increase comfort and prevent injury.
  • 20 lock-out points ensure safety during workouts.
  • Heavy-duty 12 gauge steel construction with 7 gauge reinforcement.
  • Lifetime warranty on all parts.

Dimensions

  • Width: 85"
  • Height: 82"
  • Depth: 72"

Optional Attachments

  • Selectorized Lat/Row station
  • Pec Dec station
  • Dip station
  • Plate tree
  • Accessory organizer
  • Workout DVD & Poster
